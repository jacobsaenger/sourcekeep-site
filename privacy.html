Privacy Policy

Sourcekeep transcribes on your iPhone. We don’t collect your recordings, your transcripts, or anything else about you.

The short version

  • No data is collected by us. Sourcekeep has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting of its own.
  • Your audio and transcripts stay on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Something leaves your iPhone only when you send it, by exporting or sharing a transcript yourself.

Who this is from

Sourcekeep is made by Jacob Saenger (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Sourcekeep app for iPhone and this website. Questions: jacob.w.saenger@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Recordings and transcripts

Audio you record or import, the transcripts made from it, speaker names, bookmarks, folders and your list of names and spellings are stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. iOS encrypts that storage when your iPhone is protected by a passcode.

Speech recognition

Live recording uses Apple’s speech recognition in on-device-only mode. If on-device recognition isn’t available, Sourcekeep never uses Apple’s servers instead: it doesn’t start, or, partway through a recording, it keeps recording on the iPhone and transcribes the audio there when you stop. Imported files, and the improved transcript made after a recording, are processed by open-source speech models built into the app. Where Apple Intelligence is turned on, session titles are suggested by Apple’s on-device language model. All of this runs on your iPhone.

Permissions

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: to record and transcribe. iOS’s own permission message says speech data may be sent to Apple; that describes apps that use Apple’s servers, which Sourcekeep does not.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you when a long transcription has finished, or when a recording needs you (nothing has been heard for a minute, or the iPhone is running out of space). The notification is created on your iPhone.
  • Photos and Files: only the recording you pick to import.
  • Face ID (optional): if you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. Sourcekeep only learns whether it was you; it never sees face or fingerprint data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app makes no network requests of its own, so there is no information about you or your use of the app for us to receive, sell or share.

What Apple handles

Purchases

Sourcekeep Pro is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. Apple tells us that a purchase happened and gives us sales totals, not what you record. Apple’s handling of your information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Backups

Like other apps, Sourcekeep’s data is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) if you have them turned on. Those backups are yours and are governed by your Apple settings. If your work requires it, turn backups off or exclude Sourcekeep from iCloud Backup.

App Store and TestFlight diagnostics

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports. If you test a beta through TestFlight and send feedback, Apple passes us that feedback, including the email address you use for TestFlight. None of this contains your recordings or transcripts.

When you share or export

Exporting a transcript, or every session at once with Export Everything, makes a file on your iPhone and opens the iOS share sheet; Sourcekeep deletes its copy once the share sheet closes. Where it goes next is your choice, and from that point the app or service you send it to applies its own policy. Before exporting, you can replace names with pseudonyms or redact details, and you can delete a session’s audio while keeping its transcript.

Deleting your data

In Settings you can have recordings deleted automatically 7, 30 or 90 days after they were made; this happens the first time Sourcekeep is opened after that (it can’t run while the app is closed), and their transcripts stay. Deleting a session in Sourcekeep removes its transcript and its recording. “Delete Recording, Keep Transcript” removes only the audio. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Recording other people

You are responsible for having permission to record. Laws on recording conversations differ between places, and research and journalism have their own consent rules. Sourcekeep does not tell other people that it is recording.

Children

Sourcekeep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem. Please don’t send recordings or transcripts that contain other people’s personal information.

This website

These pages use no cookies, analytics or trackers and load nothing from third parties. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

If Sourcekeep’s handling of data ever changes, this page will change first and its date will be updated. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
