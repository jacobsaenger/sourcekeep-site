Recording

Which languages does Sourcekeep transcribe?

English. Live recording uses iOS’s on-device recognition, for the English of your iPhone’s region (for example British, Australian or Indian English) where iOS has it, otherwise American English; you can choose another in Settings → Transcription. The speech model built into the app for imported files is an English model.

“On-device recognition is not available for this language on this device”

Sourcekeep only uses speech recognition that runs on your iPhone. If iOS reports that on-device recognition isn’t available, Sourcekeep stops instead of sending your audio to a server. It can help to check that Dictation is turned on (Settings → General → Keyboard) and then try again. You can still import a recording: imports use the model built into the app.

“Speech recognition is turned off for this device”

Turn on Siri & Dictation in Settings, and allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Sourcekeep in the Settings app.

Who is speaking?

When a recording stops, Sourcekeep re-transcribes it with its built-in model and tells the voices apart from the recording, on your iPhone. Then name them in the session: tap a speaker’s name above a line to change who said it, or rename a speaker everywhere at once.

Pausing

Tap pause in the recording bar for an interruption, and resume when you’re ready; the pause isn’t counted in the recording’s times. A phone call pauses the recording for you. Recording carries on when the iPhone is locked or you switch apps.

Warnings while recording

The recording screen shows which microphone is in use. If nothing has been heard for a minute (a Bluetooth microphone that turned off, a covered microphone), or the iPhone has room for less than 10 more minutes, Sourcekeep says so on screen, and with a silent notification if you’ve allowed notifications and aren’t in the app. When you tap Record, it tells you if there isn’t room for two hours.

If the app closes during a recording

Sourcekeep saves the transcript as it goes. If the app is closed or the battery runs out before you stop, the next launch brings the recording back as a session. The audio comes back too, up to the moment the app closed.

Bookmarks

Tap the bookmark button while recording to mark the moment, or bookmark a line later while you listen. Bookmarks show as marks on the playback bar.

Starting a recording quickly

“Start Recording” is available in Shortcuts and Siri and can be assigned to the Action Button. On iOS 18 and later there is also a Lock Screen and Control Center control. Pressing it again while recording bookmarks the moment.

Importing

What can I import?

Audio or video files from Files, video from Photos, or a recording shared to Sourcekeep from another app. It is transcribed on your iPhone; nothing is uploaded. Sourcekeep keeps a small audio copy for playback, never the video.

How long does it take?

Long recordings take a few minutes. You can leave Sourcekeep while it works and, if you allow notifications, it tells you when the transcript is ready.

Files longer than 15 minutes

Importing files longer than 15 minutes is part of Sourcekeep Pro. Recording in the app has no such limit.

Files longer than 3 hours

Transcribing on the iPhone takes memory, so files longer than 3 hours can’t be imported: split them into shorter parts first. A recording made in the app that runs over 3 hours keeps its live transcript; it isn’t transcribed again when you stop, so its speakers aren’t told apart.

Reviewing and exporting

Finding something

The search field on the Sessions tab looks through every title and transcript on your iPhone. Tap a matching line to open its session at that point.

Renaming a session

Tap the title at the top of a session, or touch and hold a session in the list.

Correcting a transcript

Tap a speaker’s name above a line to change who said it. Tap the “…” on a line, or touch and hold it, to open the editor. Correcting the words, bookmarking and deleting a line are free. Splitting and merging lines, checking them off and tagging [inaudible] are part of Sourcekeep Pro.

Quoting a line

In a line’s editor, Copy Quote copies the line with where it came from, ready to paste: “…” — speaker, session, date, and the time in the recording.

Listening back

Tap the speed on the right of the player (1×) to listen at 1.25×, 1.5×, 2× or 0.75×; Sourcekeep keeps the speed for your next session. After a pause, play starts two seconds back, so you hear the end of the sentence again.

Export formats

Plain text is free. Sourcekeep Pro adds Word (laid out for MAXQDA and ATLAS.ti: each turn starts with the speaker and ends with its timestamp, so they link it to the recording and can code by speaker), an NVivo transcript (a text file of Timespan, Speaker and Content columns; tick “File includes header row” when importing), a spreadsheet (CSV), PDF, Markdown, subtitles (SRT and WebVTT) and JSON. The file is made on your iPhone and you choose where it goes.

Anonymizing

Anonymize (Pro) replaces a name or detail with a code, or redacts it, across a whole session, including the title and speaker names. It changes the transcript, not the audio; you can delete the audio and keep the transcript.

Sourcekeep Pro

What is it?

A one-time purchase, with no subscription: research exports, Anonymize, the proofreading tools (split, merge, check off), Names & spellings, and imports over 15 minutes. Recording, transcription, speakers, playback, bookmarks, fixing words and plain-text export are free.

I bought Pro but it’s locked

Open any Pro feature and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. If a purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), Pro unlocks as soon as it’s approved.

Your data

Where are my recordings?

On your iPhone, in Sourcekeep’s private storage. There is no account and no cloud copy made by us. See the Privacy Policy.

Moving to a new iPhone

Sourcekeep’s data is part of your iPhone backup. Restoring a backup, or transferring directly from your old iPhone, brings your sessions with you.

Deleting

Swipe a session to delete it and its recording. “Delete Recording, Keep Transcript” in a session’s menu removes only the audio. Deleting the app removes everything.

Locking the app

In Settings (the gear on the Sessions tab), turn on Lock with Face ID and choose how soon it locks. A recording carries on while the app is locked. If Face ID fails, your iPhone passcode unlocks it.

Deleting recordings automatically

Settings → Delete Recordings removes each recording 7, 30 or 90 days after it was made, as many ethics protocols require. It happens the first time Sourcekeep is opened after that day, since it can’t run while the app is closed. Only the audio goes; the transcript stays. A session shows the date its recording will be deleted.

Exporting everything

Settings → Export Everything makes one file with every session: transcripts as plain text and the recordings, plus Word and JSON with Sourcekeep Pro. It is made on your iPhone and you choose where it goes.

For an ethics or IRB application

The app’s Privacy & Data screen (tap “On-device” on the Record tab) has a paragraph you can copy into your application.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 15 or later. The Lock Screen and Control Center control needs iOS 18, and suggested session titles need Apple Intelligence.
